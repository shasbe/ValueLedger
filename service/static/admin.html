Productivity baselines

This is the one assumption in the product. Everything else ValueLedger reports is measured; a baseline is your estimate of what a unit of work costs a human. It is stored with its source and a low/high band, and every productivity figure is rendered with both attached — so a reader can see exactly how much the conclusion rests on the guess.

A baseline with no credible source still computes, but the report will say so in its caveats and the policy will flag it.
